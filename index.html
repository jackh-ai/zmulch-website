FAQ

Common Questions

How much mulch do I need? +
Most garden beds need about 3 inches deep. We'll give you a free quote that includes exactly how much your yard requires — no guessing.
How long does the job take? +
Most residential yards are done in 2-4 hours. Larger properties may take a full day. We'll give you a time estimate when we quote.
Do I need to be home during the job? +
Nope. As long as we have access to the yard, we'll handle everything and send you photos when we're done.
Do you haul away the old mulch? +
Yes — we clear out old, dead mulch before laying fresh. Your beds will be completely clean before we start.
What types of mulch do you offer? +
We work with all mulch types. Not sure which is right for your yard? We'll recommend the best option during your free quote.
How often should I remulch? +
Once a year is the sweet spot — usually in spring. We offer seasonal packages so your yard always looks its best without you having to think about it.
